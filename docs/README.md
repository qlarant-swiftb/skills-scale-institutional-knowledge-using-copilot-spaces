# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory is the central index for OctoAcme project management guidance. Our process follows a consistent lifecycle of initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement so teams can move from idea to delivery with clear expectations and predictable handoffs. Across every phase, we prioritize customer value, iterative delivery, and evidence-based decisions.

Work begins with a lightweight one-pager and stakeholder alignment in initiation, then moves into planning with a prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, and explicit acceptance criteria. During execution and tracking, teams use project boards, regular delivery rhythms, and structured progress updates to keep work visible and actionable. Risks are actively managed through ongoing identification, assessment, mitigation, and escalation.

Roles are clearly defined to support accountability and cross-functional delivery. Project Managers coordinate schedules, dependencies, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work; Developers design, implement, and test solutions; and QA/testing contributors validate acceptance criteria and release readiness. Stakeholders provide decision input and approvals at key checkpoints.

Quality assurance is integrated throughout the lifecycle instead of being treated as a final gate. Teams align on Definition of Done, verify changes through testing and review, and apply release readiness practices that include validation, communication, and rollback awareness. Retrospectives after milestones and releases convert lessons learned into owned improvement actions, reinforcing continuous improvement across projects.
